The Autonomous Company Simulator
Can a business operating loop run through accountable AI systems rather than disconnected tools?

Brief
Overview
What if a small company ran as a loop of accountable AI systems, not a stack of disconnected SaaS tools? Build a working slice of that loop.

What you'll build
At least 3 connected agent roles (e.g. sales, ops, finance) sharing state through a common record system
A "run-the-week" loop: agents take real actions on shared records and escalate to a human inbox
Numbers move (revenue, cost, backlog, churn) and you can replay a day
At least one scenario where the company self-corrects without human input
Deliverables
Working build (repo + live demo or recorded walkthrough)
90-second Loom of a simulated day, including one human-in-the-loop moment
Architecture snapshot: shared state, agent roles, escalation rules
A failure test: one role goes off, show the others compensating or escalating
Two-year thesis (≤300 words) on the autonomous company
Why it matters
The agent era is not "AI in apps", it is "the business runs on agents." This is what that looks like, end to end.

Requirements
Submission checklist
Public repo URL (clean clone runs)
Live demo URL (required): a working link anyone can open
90-second Loom or video walkthrough (optional, but recommended)
Notes: AI tools used, key decisions, out of scope
How you'll be measured
Weight	Criterion	What we look for
25	Conceptual clarity	It feels like a company, not a script.
25	Technical depth	Real shared state, real escalations, replayable.
20	Demo quality	A non-builder can see the company "run."
15	Failure thinking	A role failing is handled, not ignored.
15	Future thesis	Strong view on autonomous business operations.
Bonus signals
Real KPIs visible and moving
Honest about which roles still need humans
Disqualifications
A single agent pretending to be three
No shared state, just isolated prompt chains
No demo
