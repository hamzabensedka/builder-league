The Agent That Earns Trust
What would an AI agent reputation, credentials, and authority look like?

Brief
Overview
Agents will negotiate, transact, and act on behalf of people and companies. They need reputation, credentials, and scoped authority, the way humans and businesses do. Build the trust layer.

What you'll build
A trust profile per agent: history of actions, scope of authority, vouches/attestations
Verifiable credentials (signed claims about what the agent has done or is allowed to do)
A way for other agents or humans to grant, revoke, or scope trust
A demo where an agent accepts or refuses a task based on the counterparty's trust profile
Deliverables
Working build (repo + live demo or recorded walkthrough)
90-second Loom showing a trust-gated interaction
Architecture snapshot: identity → claims → verification → policy
A failure test: a malicious or spoofed agent tries to act, show how trust catches it
Two-year thesis (≤300 words) on agent identity and reputation
Why it matters
Without trust primitives, multi-agent systems collapse into spam or fraud. Whoever defines the standard wins a foundational layer.

Notes: AI tools used, key decisions, out of scope

Disqualifications

No demo of a trust-gated decision
